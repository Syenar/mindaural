# Safety, Claims, and Evidence Policy

## Hearing safety

- conservative default gain and headroom
- no sudden full-scale tones
- fade-in/fade-out defaults
- clipping warnings and hard monitor safeguard
- no false mapping from digital dBFS to acoustic dB SPL
- remind users to use comfortable volume and take breaks as appropriate

## Activity warnings

Sleep/relaxation/altered-attention designs display a concise warning not to use while driving, operating machinery, or performing tasks requiring full situational awareness.

## Photosensitive visual stimulation

Visual flicker is opt-in Labs functionality with a photosensitive-seizure warning before first activation, obvious persistent stop control plus Escape-key emergency stop on keyboard-capable devices, no autoplay, and conservative defaults. Browser timing/display behavior is not represented as medical/lab calibrated.

## Clinical claims

Research summaries may accurately report published outcomes, including study population/protocol/limitations. Product marketing and community cards cannot turn those findings into diagnosis/treatment guarantees.

The first-wave product is a creation/listening/education/research-support audio application and does not make medical indications. Diagnosis, treatment, cure, mitigation, or prevention claims are outside this product specification. A medically indicated product would be a separate future product scope rather than an unfinished requirement of this release.

## Community moderation

Health or extraordinary claims can remain as clearly attributed user text only when policy permits, but they cannot earn scientific badges without qualifying sources. Search/ranking should not boost sensational claims over evidence quality.

## “Subliminal” content

Support scheduled/low-level spoken-message tracks as an audio-editing feature. Do not promise that hidden or barely audible speech produces behavioral programming. Use neutral naming such as “spoken-message track” outside legacy imports.


## Research-data privacy

Research-mode participant responses and event logs remain local/export-only by default. The community backend does not automatically synchronize participant datasets. Any future workflow that intentionally handles regulated health/research data requires a separate privacy/compliance design rather than silently reusing community storage.
