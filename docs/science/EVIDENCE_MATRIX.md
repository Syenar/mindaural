# Evidence Matrix and Claim Policy

## Evidence categories

| Level | Meaning | Allowed product language |
|---|---|---|
| Established Percept | Binaural beat as a psychoacoustic percept from dichotic frequency differences | “Produces a binaural-beat percept under suitable listening conditions.” |
| Neural Response Observed | Peer-reviewed work reports beat-related steady-state/frequency-following/cortical responses | “Beat-related neural responses have been observed in this protocol/literature.” |
| Promising Outcome Evidence | Multiple controlled studies/review/meta-analysis suggest benefit for a specific outcome, with important limitations | “Promising evidence for…” plus limitations |
| Mixed Evidence | Credible studies disagree or systematic review finds inconsistent outcomes | “Evidence is mixed.” |
| Limited Evidence | Few/small/non-replicated studies | “Limited evidence; experimental.” |
| Experimental | Mechanism/protocol not sufficiently supported for an outcome claim | “Experimental; no established outcome.” |
| Community Claim | User-authored subjective claim | “Community-created claim; not independently verified.” |

## Rules

1. Evidence attaches to a **specific claim and protocol**, not to a naked frequency number.
2. EEG band names are descriptive frequency bands, not prescriptions. “10 Hz is alpha-range” is acceptable; “10 Hz causes relaxation” requires separate outcome evidence and still should not be stated as universal causation.
3. Imported BWGen descriptions retain historic wording only as attributed legacy metadata, clearly separated from our scientific summary.
4. No first-wave preset may claim to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate, or prevent disease. Medical indications are outside this product specification rather than a deferred release feature.
5. Claims involving neurotransmitter release, replacing sleep, IQ increases, chakra activation, telepathy, astral projection, out-of-body experiences, DNA effects, or similar extraordinary outcomes are not promoted as factual without extraordinary direct evidence. Historic/community text is labeled unverified.
6. Study-specific presets state what a study **found**, not what all users should expect.
7. Negative/null findings are eligible for Research Library presets and summaries; the library is not a marketing-only evidence filter.

## Current evidence baseline

- **Binaural percept / auditory mechanism:** established enough for accurate engineering.
- **Reliable global EEG entrainment:** mixed/unsettled. The 2023 systematic review found five supportive, eight contradictory, one mixed among 14 qualifying studies.
- **Cognition/attention/memory:** mixed. Some positive experiments/meta-analyses exist; a large 2023 home-use experiment reported worsened test performance.
- **Anxiety/pain:** promising in some settings, including perioperative evidence, but protocols/outcomes are heterogeneous. A 2026 theta-specific review included 13 randomized trials yet could pool only four pain trials (173 participants), rating that evidence low certainty; anxiety/cognition were too heterogeneous to pool. Do not generalize to treatment claims.
- **Sleep:** plausible/actively studied but do not infer efficacy from EEG-band labels alone; require protocol-specific evidence.
- **Altered states/metaphysical outcomes:** experimental/unverified for product purposes.
