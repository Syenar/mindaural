# Research Bibliography

This is a local index of sources used to build the scientific model. Re-check primary sources before making a new strong medical/clinical claim or when implementing a source-exact Research Protocol preset.

## Core reviews/meta-analyses

### Ingendoh, Posny & Heine (2023) — PLOS ONE
**Title:** Binaural beats to entrain the brain? A systematic review of the effects of binaural beat stimulation on brain oscillatory activity, and the implications for psychological research and intervention  
**DOI:** 10.1371/journal.pone.0286023  
**URL:** https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0286023  
**Local takeaway:** 14 studies; five supportive of entrainment, eight contradictory, one mixed; high methodological heterogeneity; no settled answer. Summarizes approximate conventional percept range (~1–30 Hz), carrier constraints (<~1000 Hz), around-400-Hz perceptual reference, individual variability.

### Garcia-Argibay, Santed & Reales (2019) — Psychological Research
**Title:** Efficacy of binaural auditory beats in cognition, anxiety, and pain perception: a meta-analysis  
**DOI:** 10.1007/s00426-018-1066-8  
**PMID:** 30073406  
**URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30073406/  
**Local takeaway:** 22 studies, 35 effect sizes, overall Hedges' g=0.45; timing/duration moderators reported. Positive aggregate signal, but heterogeneous domains/protocols prevent universal frequency prescriptions.

### Xiong et al. (2025) — Complementary Therapies in Medicine
**Title:** Binaural beats for perioperative anxiety and pain: A systematic review and meta-analysis  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.ctim.2025.103299  
**PMID:** 41176178  
**URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41176178/  
**Local takeaway:** 15 randomized trials; anxiety/pain benefits reported versus controls; anxiety heterogeneity was very high (I² >90% in key comparisons), so product language must emphasize promise + heterogeneity rather than certainty.


### Fatima et al. (2026) — Explore
**Title:** Efficacy of theta binaural beat therapy on pain, cognition and anxiety in adults: A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials  
**PMID:** 42349368  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.explore.2026.103471  
**URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42349368/  
**Local takeaway:** 13 randomized controlled trials of theta-frequency binaural beats were included. Only four trials (173 participants) contributed to the quantitative pain meta-analysis; the authors rated the pain evidence low certainty. Anxiety and cognition could not be pooled because protocols/outcomes were too heterogeneous. This supports protocol-specific “promising/low-certainty” language rather than a generalized therapy claim.

## Mechanism / electrophysiology

### Orozco Perez, Dumas & Lehmann (2020) — eNeuro
**Title:** Binaural beats through the auditory pathway: From brainstem to connectivity patterns  
**PMID:** 32066611  
**URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7082494/  
**Local takeaway:** controlled comparison of binaural/monaural stimulation; carrier-related subcortical and beat-related cortical responses; connectivity differences; no simple mood benefit; monaural entrainment can be stronger in some conditions. Example protocols use exact dichotic pure tones and calibrated headphone presentation.

### Ross & Lopez (2020) — Scientific Reports
**Title:** 40-Hz Binaural beats enhance training to mitigate the attentional blink  
**DOI:** 10.1038/s41598-020-63980-y  
**URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-63980-y  
**Local takeaway:** MEG showed stronger gamma entrainment under 40 Hz than 16 Hz; no within-session performance improvement, but a between-session training pattern favored 40 Hz. Useful example of why timing and outcome definition matter.

## Parametric / contradictory outcome studies

### Melnichuk, Cooper & Hawk (2025) — Scientific Reports
**Title:** A parametric investigation of binaural beats for brain entrainment and enhancing sustained attention  
**DOI:** 10.1038/s41598-025-88517-z  
**URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-88517-z  
**Local takeaway:** 80 participants; factorial manipulation of beta/gamma, 340/400 Hz carrier, timing, masking noise; EEG entrainment and behavior depended on parameters. Do not hard-code 400 Hz as universally optimal.

### Klichowski et al. (2023) — Scientific Reports
**Title:** Reverse effect of home-use binaural beats brain stimulation  
**DOI:** 10.1038/s41598-023-38313-4  
**URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-38313-4  
**Local takeaway:** large home-use study (~1000 participants across studies) reported worse fluid-intelligence task performance under binaural beats. Strong reason to include null/negative evidence and avoid “focus booster” guarantees.
