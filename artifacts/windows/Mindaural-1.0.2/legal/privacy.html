Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026 · Publisher and developer: Syenar

Mindaural is a local-first audio workstation. This policy explains what information the website and application handle.

Summary

  • Mindaural does not sell personal information.
  • Audio files, projects, presets, and analysis data remain on your device unless you intentionally use a sharing or cloud feature.
  • No account is required to use the demo or local workstation.
  • The demo uses browser audio synthesis and does not record your microphone.

Information stored locally

The app may store settings, imported audio metadata, projects, playlists, presets, session history, and cached application files in browser storage or the application data directory. These are controlled by your device and may be removed by clearing site data or uninstalling the application. Imported audio is processed locally by default.

Optional network features

If a future or enabled build offers cloud libraries, community sharing, update checks, or support links, those actions are user initiated and may transmit the information required for that action. The applicable screen will identify the destination and data involved. The public website may have ordinary server logs handled by its hosting provider.

Support correspondence

If you email support, we receive your email address and message so we can respond and keep a reasonable record of the request. We use that information for support and do not sell it.

Children

Mindaural is not designed to collect personal information from children. No account is required. Parents and guardians should supervise use in accordance with local law.

Your choices and rights

You can delete local app data by clearing browser site data or uninstalling the desktop app. If you contact us by email, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or other rights available under applicable law for personal information in that correspondence.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when material updates are made.
